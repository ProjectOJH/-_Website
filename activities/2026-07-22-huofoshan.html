What If a Mountain Held More Than Just Views?
山海能量．一日侑 · ONE-DAY MOUNTAIN & SEA ENERGY JOURNEY
📅 日期 · Date 2026.07.22（三） 🕤 活動時間 · Time 09:30－17:30 📍 集合地點 · Meeting Point 金山．萬里（依行前通知）

如果，一座山不只是風景呢？如果它承載著古老的故事、土地的智慧，以及流傳至今的能量呢？

邀請您一起走進活佛山，不只是欣賞山林之美，更從風水地理、歷史文化與土地能量的角度， 重新認識這片土地，以及自己。

無論您對風水地理、文化歷史感興趣，或只是想安排一天放慢腳步、親近自然，這趟旅程，都值得您親自體驗。

What if a mountain held more than just breathtaking views? What if it also carried stories, ancient wisdom, and the energy of the land itself?

Join us for a one-day journey to Living Buddha Mountain (活佛山), where we'll explore not only its natural beauty, but also the culture, history, Feng Shui, and the deeper connection between people and the places they inhabit.

Whether you're curious about Feng Shui, fascinated by culture, or simply looking for a meaningful day in nature, this journey invites you to experience the mountain from a different perspective.
